4 · Poisoning-resistance sweep

Source: poisoning_resistance_sweep.csv. Full pipeline, 20 rounds, seed 42. “Survival” = share of attacker-rounds in which an attacker passed the trust filter into aggregation.

5 · Multi-seed check on the 20%-vs-30% anomaly

Source: multiseed_20_30_check.csv plus seed 42 from the sweep. At seed 42 the 20% point had lower AE-alone recall than 30%; the ordering reverses at seed 43, so it is noise, not a trend.

6 · Zero-day (leave-one-attack-class-out)

Source: zero_day_holdout.csv. 0% attackers, 20 rounds, seed 42. Each class is removed from the calibration set, every client's data and every scaler, then scored as unseen traffic (up to 5,000 rows). MITM has 406 rows and Fingerprinting 853.
